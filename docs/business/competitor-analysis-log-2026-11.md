# Competitor analysis: running log

Started 17 November 2026. Question: who are TapWater.uk's five main competitors, what do they do that we do not, and which ten changes are most likely to make money.

Entries are in the order the work happened. The finished report is `competitor-analysis-2026-11.md` in this folder.

## 1. Starting point (from the audit earlier today)

- 5,213 Search Console clicks in 28 days; postcode pages get 16% of them but produce half the affiliate clicks and 42 of 47 softener leads.
- Estimated income about £100 a month, all affiliate. Leads were never sold; the installer marketplace went live today with no installers yet.
- Buying guides sit on pages 3 to 5 of Google, except under-sink filters (position 3.8).
- Town-shaped hardness searches land on postcode pages at positions 50 to 80.

## 2. Method

1. Search the queries that matter to us and note which sites hold the top places.
2. Pick the five that compete for the same visitor and the same money, not just the same words.
3. For each: what they rank for, how the visitor is turned into money, what the offer and price are, how they sign up partners or customers, what they send by email.
4. Compare with our own numbers. Rank changes by expected money, with the evidence beside each.

- **Our Taap**: a filter-system maker (subscription or outright) using a postcode "Water Confidence Index" as the way in.

## 4. The five chosen

Chosen because each takes the same visitor and a kind of money we want:

1. **MyTapWater**: same product as ours, already charging consumers and businesses for it.
2. **TapWaterData**: same product, competing for the same rankings with affiliate and email.
3. **Harvey Water Softeners**: owns the hardness-checker-to-softener-sale route we are trying to sell leads into.
4. **MyJobQuote** (HaMuch noted alongside): sells softener leads to tradespeople, the model we launched today.
5. **Our Taap**: postcode checker as the front door to its own subscription product.

Not chosen, kept as context: Aqua Cure and UK Water Filters (same pattern as Harvey, smaller), water companies (not selling anything), review publishers (compete only for buying guides, which are not where our money is).

## 8. Researcher reports, as they came in

### 8.1 TapWaterData (tapwaterdata.com/uk)

- **Size:** a large US site (about 71,500 URLs) with a UK section bolted on in September 2026: 1,404 postcode pages and 100 other UK pages. No company name, address or person on the about page; "TapWaterData LLC" appears on its app page.
- **Money in the UK: close to none.** Three products in total, all linking to amazon.com (US store, US fittings), no prices shown; its own page warns the under-sink pick "may not ship to the UK". No softener quote route, no installer referral, no test-kit pick.
- **Money elsewhere:** newsletter sponsorship rate card ($300 an issue, $900 for four) against a published list of 4,727 subscribers at 36.9% open rate; a US-only "book a free call" with a treatment specialist on revenue share; its own hydration app.
- **Traffic hooks we lack:** 20 "Is there a [company] hosepipe ban right now?" pages with a separate "Get UK hosepipe news" signup on every postcode page; storm-overflow counts on every postcode page ("N overflows within 10 km") with company rankings.
- **Weak spots:** hosepipe status last checked 11 October (37 days old) with five big companies blank; no hardness figure for the whole Thames Water area; hard and soft areas get the same two products.
- **Where it showed in searches:** "is Manchester tap water safe to drink" (town page). Not for postcode hardness or hosepipe searches.
- Source: researcher's page reads of /uk, /uk/filters, /uk/postcode/rg1, /uk/postcode/m1, /uk/hosepipe-ban, /uk/sewage, /partners, /uk/our-data and the sitemap files.

### 8.2 Our Taap (ourtaap.com)

- **Who it really is:** Virgin Pure renamed in March 2025. Companies House number 04880825, majority-owned by Strauss Water (Israel), with Culligan holding between 25% and 50%. Culligan also owns Harvey.
- **Products:** plumbed-in water system on hire at £35.99 a month for 24 months (£37.99 for 12, £39.99 rolling), or £499.98 outright; ChillPro from £24.99 a month; MiniHot £99.99. Engineer-installed, credit check required, customer owes the rest of the contract if they cancel.
- **Affiliate programme we can join:** Awin merchant 122454, fixed bounty per subscription. Two Awin pages disagree: £60 or £80 for a 24-month plan, £30 or £40 for 12 months, £10 rolling, 3% on outright purchases. 30-day cookie.
- **Its postcode checker:** score out of 100, no email asked for, generic "shop the range" afterwards, no method published. Data "supplied by MyTapWater".
- **What MyTapWater charges for data:** its public API price list is £25, £199, £599 and £1,199 a month by lookup volume; Our Taap is the only named customer. What Our Taap actually pays was not found.
- **Trust:** Trustpilot 4.8 from 9,990 reviews (mostly inherited from Virgin Pure), Good Housekeeping 96/100.
- **Weak spots:** its own pages contradict each other on price, what is included and warranty; no independent review ranks for "our taap review".
- **Other programmes noted:** Waterdrop UK 10% (Awin 117649, which we already use), ZeroWater UK 8% and Coway up to 7.5% (search snippets, not verified).
- Not found: what Our Taap pays MyTapWater, accounts figures, paid social, retail partners.

### 8.3 MyJobQuote, with HaMuch, Bark and Checkatrade for comparison

Limits: HaMuch blocked every direct page read, so its details come from search snippets and Trustpilot. Bark's and Checkatrade's help pages were also blocked; Bark figures come from a third party (Whito) that says it read them.

- **MyJobQuote, homeowner side:** one national guide, "Water Softener Installation Cost", headline "The average cost to install a water softener is £1,250", repeated "compare up to 3 quotes" buttons. No softener-by-town pages.
- **MyJobQuote, trade side:** credits per lead, pay-as-you-go or monthly membership with a free month; one free lead on adding a card. Prices are not published. One trade review gives "50 credits for £82" and a 24-credit lead, about £39 (single data point, job type unknown); another site quotes leads "from £7 to £15". Credits expire after 6 months. Bad-lead disputes only within 48 hours, refunded as credits at their discretion. A lead goes to up to 3 trades.
- **Trade complaints (Trustpilot 1-star, Nov 2026):** fake or recycled leads, customers who never answer, the 48-hour dispute window, payments taken after cancelling.
- **HaMuch (snippets only):** softener-fitting pages by town, postcode district and even street, each with a local price in the title ("...in your Twickenham home from £2,485"). Trades pay a flat monthly fee, reported as about £20 to £30; several say they were listed without consent and with wrong prices.
- **Bark (third-party):** £1.80 plus VAT per credit; a plumbing lead reported at roughly £14 to £22 plus VAT; up to 5 professionals per lead; credits expire after 3 months.
- **Checkatrade (its pricing page):** £0, £30 or from £59 a month on 12-month memberships; no per-lead fee.
- **Benchmarks:** MyBuilder shortlist fees "£2 to £35"; Boiler Guide leads from £6 with at most three companies. No platform publishes a softener lead price or a lead-to-job rate.
- **Researcher's view on our offer:** £35 a lead shared by at most 2 is at the top of the observed range but defensible (2.8% of a £1,250 job). Keep the price; publish a replacement rule longer than 48 hours, say credits never expire, state whether VAT is included, put hardness and household details on every lead, consider a dearer exclusive tier.
- Not found: an actual softener lead price on any platform, HaMuch's page count and current fee.

### 8.4 Harvey Water Softeners (Culligan Harvey)

- **The checker:** "How Hard Is Your Water?" gives one parts-per-million figure with no supplier, zone or date; where it has no data it says "you could still benefit from a water softener". The "UK hard water map" address serves the same page.
- **What it asks for:** every route ends in a phone-required form for a free in-home water test and demo ("usually about an hour"), with "slots are limited and filling fast!" pop-ups. No newsletter, no brochure, no email-only step.
- **Prices are hidden.** The only public figures: "Basic installation packages start from £1,195" on a footer facts page, and a £2,300 finance example (0% over 12 months, 11.9% APR over 3 or 5 years). Current offer: £150 off until 30 November 2026.
- **No local pages.** 138 pages and 168 posts, four place-related, none for towns. Did not appear for "water softener Reading".
- **It already takes referred customers from other businesses.** Co-branded "trusted affiliate" pages exist for Schmidt, Mockford & Hunt and Wright Clean, each with a promo code and a year's free salt for the customer. What the referrer is paid was not found.
- **Coverage:** bookings "restricted strictly to core coverage areas (e.g., London and Home Counties)".
- **Dealers who sell its TwinTec range:** UK Water Softeners (Wokingham, read on its own site), Thames Valley Softeners (Wokingham) and West London Water Softeners (Kingston) from search snippets, Aqua Sensation (Letchworth, read on Which? Trusted Traders). Three of these are already on our outreach list. None found for north or west London.
- **Trust:** Trustpilot 4.6 from about 14,800 reviews, many about salt deliveries. 3-year guarantee on Harvey-branded units against 10 years on dealer-sold TwinTec.
- Not found: any affiliate network programme, what referral partners earn, monthly rental price. The Harvey shop was blocked.

### 8.5 MyTapWater (mytapwater.co.uk, Hyde Organics Ltd)

Read from the live domain; postcode results read in a browser.

- **Size:** 312 indexable pages: about 105 postcode districts (of roughly 2,900), 71 town pages, 25 contaminants, 23 companies. Full-postcode results are drawn by script at `/?postcode=`, so they are not separate pages. Two `pages.dev` copies are indexed and outrank the main domain in the searches run.
- **Paid reports:** "Unlock your full water report … £19.99 / 6-page PDF" sits directly under the free score, with the postcode pre-filled into a hosted checkout (Lemon Squeezy). A £39.99 Property Water Report (nine sections, said to be 15 pages, "emailed within 1 working day") is aimed at buyers, landlords and conveyancers. Both are postcode-level. No reviews or testimonials; no evidence either way of how many sell.
- **Business prices:** API £25, £199, £599, £1,199 a month (500 to 100,000 lookups); widget £99, £249, £499, from £999. No free tier. Only named customer: Our Taap.
- **Affiliate:** AquaTru through Impact with a negotiated "save up to £95" partner price, placed straight after the PFAS notice; Waterdrop reference links; Amazon search links for softeners.
- **No softener quote route.** Its hard-water page says it "does not sell water softeners". Hard and soft postcodes get the same money path.
- **Email:** "Local water alerts" signup for incidents and hosepipe bans on the homepage and news page only.
- **Company:** incorporated 6 June 2024, one director, only dormant accounts filed (to June 2025). No named people on the site, one unlinked press mention, no Trustpilot page.
- **Weak spots:** hardness figures contradict each other between page types (SW10: 318 on the district page, 252 in the live result); 79% of postcodes score "Fair" by its own method; static pages show "Looking up your postcode…" with no data; latest news item 23 July 2026.
- **Where it showed in searches:** first for a postcode quality search and a lead-pipe checker search (both via the mirrors); absent for "is Manchester tap water safe" and for house-purchase water report searches.

## 11. How the ranking was made

Each candidate change was scored on three things: the money it could bring in the next six months (with the arithmetic shown), how strong the evidence is (our own numbers first, a competitor doing it second, a researcher's inference last), and how much of it depends on someone outside the business saying yes. Estimates are mine and are marked as estimates. The result is in `competitor-analysis-2026-11.md`.
