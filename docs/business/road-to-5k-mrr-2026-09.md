Written 14 September 2026. Companion to `docs/seo/organic-recovery-plan-2026-09.md`.

## The arithmetic at £5k

| Engine | Monthly | How |
|---|---|---|
| Softener and hard-water leads | £3,000 | 60 leads at £50 through a direct installer deal. Bark at £25 to £40 as the bridge. |
| Fixed-bounty affiliates and sponsorship | £1,200 | 15 Osmio-class sales at £60, plus one sponsored guide at £300. |
| B2B water data | £800 | 4 customers at £200 a month for an address-level feed or widget. |

Traffic needed: ~35,000 visits a month, ~8,000 of them on hard-water pages converting 0.75% to a lead. That is a 13x traffic increase and a 4x increase in revenue per visit. Both have to happen.

## Three engines

### 1. Lead generation (the main engine)
Hard water is the only problem on the site people will pay to solve, and installers pay £50 to £75 for a lead with a phone number. The funnel already exists on postcode, city, region and supplier pages and in the email drip. It needs a partner, then volume, then a direct contract.

### 2. Affiliate that actually pays
Amazon at 3% on jugs cannot get there. Every rail moves fixed-bounty products (Osmio, direct programmes) ahead of Amazon. Once a guide has 10,000 visits a month, one filter brand pays a flat monthly fee to be featured. Dashboards are reconciled monthly against the click log so the real number is known.

### 3. B2B water data
Property portals, letting agents, conveyancers and home-buyer report providers want "what is the water like at this address". The data, the postcode lookup and the embed widget already exist. Package it as a feed and a widget with a price, run three free pilots, convert them. This is the one engine that does not wait for Google.

### The prerequisite: traffic
None of the engines pays without visitors. The recovery plan covers it: disavow, thin pages gone (done), unique district content (done), monthly data stories to regional press, widget outreach for links, forum answers, and the softener cluster ranking. Target DR 25 by mid-2027.

## Weekly rhythm
- Monday: numbers. Clicks, leads, revenue, GSC, one sentence on what moved.
- Every week: one growth action shipped, not two half-done.
- Monthly: reconcile every partner dashboard against the click log.

## Who does what
- Remy: every conversation with a partner, installer, prospect, journalist or sponsor. Account access, uploads, applications. This is the bottleneck, and it is a sales job.
- Claude: build, content, data, outreach drafts, analysis, weekly numbers.

## Decision points
- 31 March 2027: if GSC is still under 900 clicks a week and there is no direct installer deal, SEO-led lead-gen is failing. B2B data becomes the primary engine.
- 30 September 2027: if revenue is under £1,000 a month, the site is a side asset. Stop investing time beyond maintenance.

## Risks
- Google decides the recovery timing, not us.
- Lead quality: installers refund leads without a phone number or outside their area. The form already requires a phone.
- The direct installer deal needs volume first, which is why Bark comes first even though it pays less.
- B2B sales cycles run months, and every conversation is Remy's.
- One person doing all outbound is the ceiling on speed.
