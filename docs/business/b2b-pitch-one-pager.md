# TapWater.uk data: one-page pitch

**What it is.** The water report behind 2,242 UK postcode pages, as an API. Quality score, hardness, readings over the legal limit, PFAS, live supply incidents, supplier, provenance. One call per address, JSON, key in a header.

**Where the data comes from.** Water company drinking-water test results published through the Stream Water Data Portal, Environment Agency river and groundwater monitoring (12,000 PFAS detections), and water company incident feeds refreshed every fifteen minutes. Every report links to a public page showing the same figures.

**Who buys it.** Property portals and letting software (a water line on every listing), home-buyer report providers (a water section in the pack), conveyancing search providers (a missing environmental layer), boiler cover and appliance brands (scale risk by address), filter and softener sellers (qualify the lead).

**Pricing.** Pilot free for 90 days at 1,000 lookups a month. Starter £99 a month for 5,000. Growth £249 a month for 50,000 plus a monthly CSV of every district. Enterprise custom. Same-day repeats of a postcode are free.

**Why now.** UK water is in the news every month: PFAS, sewage, hosepipe bans, hardness and limescale costs. Nobody else packages the published tests by address.
