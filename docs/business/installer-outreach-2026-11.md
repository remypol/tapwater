## Before you send

- Send from `partners@tapwater.uk`, the address the installer page gives, so replies land where the page says they will. Check that mailbox is actually monitored.
- Send them one at a time from your own mail client, not as a bulk mail-out. Each one names the installer's own area and numbers.
- Emailing a limited company at its business address about something relevant to its trade is normally allowed in the UK, provided you say who you are and offer a way to opt out (both drafts do). A sole trader counts as an individual under the same rules, so do not cold-email Thames Valley Softeners: phone instead. This is my reading of the rules, not legal advice.
- Two installers per request is the cap. Compass and UK Water Softeners both want RG; that is fine and is what the cap is for, but do not promise either of them exclusivity.

## When someone signs up

1. You get an email titled "New installer to approve". Check they are real (website, Companies House), then open the link and press the button.
2. They immediately receive the open requests in their areas, up to their three free leads, and each homeowner is told who will call.
3. Card top-ups work since 17 November 2026: "Add leads" on the installer's account page opens a Stripe payment page for £175 in TapWater.uk's name, and the five leads are added when it is paid. The money arrives in the shared "SFM Studios" Stripe account (the one Invoice Navigator and ChemTracker use), marked "TapWater.uk installer leads".
