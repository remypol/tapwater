## What would count as working

- Report test: more than about 30 clicks and 5 signups in two weeks is worth building the PDF for. Fewer than 10 clicks means the placement or the price is wrong, not necessarily the idea.
- Alerts: more than 15 new subscribers in two weeks, against about 3 or 4 before.
- Town pages: any page ranking in the top 10 for "water hardness {town}" within four weeks; a request with source `hardness_town`.
- Cost guide: position better than 20 for "water softener cost" within six weeks.

These thresholds are judgment calls, set before seeing any result.
